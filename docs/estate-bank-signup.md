# Estate bank signup preparation

Verified October 12, 2026. Application preparation only; no bank account has been opened or funded.

## Recommendation

Use Ally estate Spending for probate operating payments, with optional estate Savings for reserves. Ally is the simpler default while the credit-union estate membership basis is unresolved. Confirm Ally accepts a standalone estate application and the appointed representative's residency and court authority. Ally's official estate form supports new customers and an enclosed funding check, so a prior Ally relationship is not assumed to be necessary; confirm this specific case with an estate specialist.

Quorum Probate Express becomes preferable if its membership gate is cleared and opening speed is the priority. Quorum advertises an online application and opening in 24 hours or less with complete correct information. That is conditional, not guaranteed. NCUA's published estate opinion says the deceased or all beneficiaries must be members; the administrator's own membership does not alone establish estate eligibility. Confirm Quorum's implementation before enrolling beneficiaries, especially any overseas beneficiaries.

## Prepare signup

1. Verify the exact estate name, appointed representative, court/case, current Letters and restrictions. Use the estate EIN, not the decedent or administrator personal tax ID as the estate taxpayer.
2. Gather the death certificate, court appointment/Letters, IRS estate EIN letter, representative ID and residential/contact details. Enter SSN/ITIN, security answers and other sensitive fields only in the bank's secure process.
3. Call Ally at **1-877-247-2559** (24/7). Request an estate specialist and secure upload link for a standalone estate Spending application; add Savings only when a reserve balance justifies it.
4. Confirm exact account title, co-representative signing powers, residency, accepted documents, funding method, estate-payable check endorsement, mobile-deposit limits, new-account holds and transfer limits.
5. Complete the estate application, tax certifications and permitted signature. Ally accepts wet ink or a placed digital signature; it rejects typed signatures. Submit only after the identity and authority fields are verified.
6. Retain submission and approval evidence privately. Confirm account title and estate EIN before funding. Deposit only money payable to and belonging to the estate; named-beneficiary insurance and retirement money are not automatically probate funds.
7. Pay authorized expenses from cleared funds; retain invoices, receipts and a reconciled ledger. Opening a deposit account does not authorize a new credit-card application.

For Quorum instead, call **800-874-5544**, Monday-Friday 8:30 a.m.-7 p.m. Eastern, before using the application. Confirm deceased/beneficiary membership, non-US beneficiary treatment, current estate APY, minimum deposit, transaction-fee exemptions and court restrictions.

Estate deposits generally have $250,000 aggregate federal insurance at one institution, including applicable decedent balances. More beneficiaries or multiple products do not multiply coverage. Confirm arrangements before placing more than this amount, including any approved sweep network.
