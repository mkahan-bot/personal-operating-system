CBD mg per bottle vs. mg per mL

Display both numbers everywhere. 600 mg per 60 mL = 10 mg/mL. 1200 mg per 60 mL = 20 mg/mL.

How to read a COA

Look for product name, lot number, sample date, lab identity, CBD result, total THC result, method limits and contaminant panels.

Questions for your veterinarian

Ask about age, size, health conditions, medications and product suitability before using any CBD product for a pet.

CBD oil vs. hemp seed oil

Hemp seed oil and CBD extract are not interchangeable. Identify the source of CBD, carrier oil and exact CBD concentration.

FAQ

Answer the hard questions clearly.

A trustworthy pet CBD site should not bury FDA status, THC testing, shipping restrictions or veterinary guidance.

Has FDA approved CBD for pets?

No. The draft site should state that FDA has not approved CBD for use in animals and should avoid disease-treatment claims.

Does Millie's treat anxiety, pain or arthritis?

No. Launch copy should not claim to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate or prevent diseases.

Can Millie's ship everywhere?

No universal shipping promise should be made before a state-by-state legal matrix is complete. Checkout should block restricted jurisdictions before payment.

Prelaunch gate: Final formula, state-by-state shipping rules, CBD payment processing, insurance, label review and claims review must be completed before live sales.
